How does Amanapay protect buyers?
Funds are held in a secure independent vault. The vendor only receives payment after you inspect and confirm delivery.
What happens to the 3.0% fee if a refund occurs?
The 3.0% platform fee covers secure gateway processing and escrow management and is non-refundable upon payment execution. The principal item amount is fully returned.
How do I raise a dispute?
Go to your receipt view after paying, click "Raise a Dispute", input your M-Pesa receipt code and reason, and submit it to freeze funds.
